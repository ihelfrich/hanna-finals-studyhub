# ACCT 410x: Final Exam "Quick Strike" Cheat Sheet

## I. The Fundamental Pillars
*   **The Accounting Equation:** $Assets = Liabilities + Equity$
    *   *Equity Components:* $Contributed Capital + Retained Earnings$
    *   *Retained Earnings:* $Beginning~RE + Net~Income - Dividends$
    *   *Net Income:* $Revenues - Expenses$
*   **Normal Balances:**
    *   **Debit (Increase):** Assets, Dividends, Expenses (**ADE**)
    *   **Credit (Increase):** Liabilities, Equity, Revenue (**LER**)

---

## II. Financial Statement Analysis (Ratios)
### Liquidity (Short-term health)
*   **Current Ratio:** $Current Assets / Current Liabilities$
*   **Inventory Turnover:** $COGS / Average Inventory$

### Profitability (Efficiency)
*   **Profit Margin:** $Net Income / Net Sales$
*   **Return on Assets (ROA):** $Net Income / Average Total Assets$

### Solvency (Long-term health)
*   **Debt to Assets:** $Total Liabilities / Total Assets$

---

## III. Inventory & Operating Assets
*   **COGS Equation:** $Beginning~Inventory + Purchases - Ending~Inventory = COGS$
*   **Cost Flow Assumptions:**
    *   **FIFO:** First-in, First-out (Ending Inventory reflects *current* costs).
    *   **LIFO:** Last-in, First-out (Ending Inventory reflects *oldest* costs).
*   **Depreciation (Straight-Line):** $(Cost - Salvage~Value) / Useful~Life$

---

## IV. Managerial Accounting (CVP & Decisions)
*   **Contribution Margin (CM):** $Sales - Variable~Costs$
*   **CM Ratio:** $Unit~CM / Unit~Sales~Price$ (or $Total~CM / Total~Sales$)
*   **Break-Even Point (Units):** $Fixed~Costs / Unit~CM$
*   **Target Profit (Units):** $(Fixed~Costs + Target~Profit) / Unit~CM$
*   **Relevant Costs for Decisions:**
    *   **Ignore:** Sunk Costs (past costs) and Allocated Fixed Costs (that don't change).
    *   **Include:** Incremental Revenues, Variable Costs, and Opportunity Costs.

---

## V. Statement of Cash Flows (Indirect Method)
*   **Start:** Net Income
*   **Add back:** Depreciation Expense / Amortization.
*   **Losses/Gains:** Add back **Losses**; Subtract **Gains**.
*   **Asset Changes:** Increase in Asset = **Subtract**; Decrease in Asset = **Add**.
*   **Liability Changes:** Increase in Liability = **Add**; Decrease in Liability = **Subtract**.
