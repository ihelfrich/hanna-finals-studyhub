## Part III: Applied Problem B - Incremental Analysis (25 Points)
**Scenario:** *Catalina Water Taxi* operates a fleet of ferries. They are considering a "Special Order" from a local university for a weekend excursion.

**Standard Product Data (Per Trip):**
*   Selling Price: $2,500
*   Variable Costs (Fuel, Crew, Supplies): $1,200
*   Fixed Costs (Allocated Dock Fees, Insurance): $800

**Special Order Details:**
*   The university offers $1,500 per trip for 5 trips.
*   The order would use existing excess capacity (no impact on regular customers).
*   The order would require a one-time "Port Permit Fee" of $500.

**Task:**
1.  Identify which costs are **relevant** for this decision.
2.  Calculate the **Incremental Net Income** if the order is accepted.
3.  State whether the order should be accepted and **justify** your answer using "User-Oriented" logic.
