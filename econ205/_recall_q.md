# ECON 205 Final Exam — Active Recall Bank (60 Q)

Cumulative. Roughly 30% pre-midterm refresher, 70% post-midterm. Answer cold, then check the key.

## Section A — Pre-Midterm Refresher (15 questions)

1. State the law of demand in one sentence.
2. Distinguish a movement along demand from a shift of demand.
3. Define a binding price ceiling and state its predicted effect.
4. Write the midpoint formula for own-price elasticity of demand.
5. If $|\varepsilon_D|=0.6$, is demand elastic or inelastic, and what happens to revenue when price rises?
6. Write the spending-approach GDP identity.
7. Why are intermediate goods excluded from GDP?
8. Define real GDP and explain how it differs from nominal GDP.
9. Write the GDP-deflator formula.
10. Write all four formulas: LF, u, LFPR, EPOP.
11. Define discouraged worker and explain why u-rate can fall when they exit.
12. Write the closed-economy national-saving identity.
13. State the open-economy identity linking S, I, NX.
14. In $Y=AK^{0.5}L^{0.5}$, by what factor does Y change if K alone doubles? What concept?
15. State two policies that raise long-run productivity.

## Section B — Money & Inflation (Ch. 11) — 6 questions

16. Write the quantity equation and define each variable.
17. Under stable V and Y, what is the implied relationship between money growth and inflation?
18. Write the exact Fisher equation and the approximation.
19. If i = 6% and π^e = 3%, what is the real interest rate?
20. List three costs of expected inflation.
21. Who gains and who loses from unexpected inflation between creditors and debtors?

## Section C — Economic Fluctuations & AD/AS (Ch. 12–14) — 9 questions

22. List three reasons AD slopes downward.
23. Why does SRAS slope upward in the short run?
24. Why is LRAS vertical?
25. Predict the short-run effect of a positive AD shock on P and Y.
26. Predict the short-run effect of an adverse supply shock on P, Y, and u.
27. Define stagflation.
28. Describe the long-run self-correction mechanism after a negative AD shock.
29. State Okun's Law as a rule of thumb.
30. What is potential output, and what determines it in the long run?

## Section D — Fiscal Policy (Ch. 15) — 8 questions

31. Write the simple closed-economy spending multiplier.
32. Write the tax multiplier.
33. State the balanced-budget multiplier and explain intuitively.
34. Define crowding out and identify its mechanism.
35. Give two automatic stabilizers and explain why they reduce volatility.
36. Distinguish deficit (flow) from debt (stock).
37. Why does the open-economy multiplier tend to be smaller than the closed-economy multiplier?
38. Under what conditions is fiscal stimulus most effective?

## Section E — Monetary Policy (Ch. 16) — 8 questions

39. List four traditional Fed policy tools.
40. Trace the transmission: Fed buys bonds → reserves → fed funds → r → AD.
41. Write the simple money multiplier with reserve ratio rr.
42. Define the Zero Lower Bound and one tool used to circumvent it.
43. State the Taylor rule in symbols.
44. Why do monetary-policy lags complicate countercyclical action?
45. What is forward guidance?
46. Distinguish IOR (interest on reserves) from the discount rate.

## Section F — Financial Markets (Ch. 17) — 5 questions

47. Explain the inverse relationship between bond prices and yields.
48. Write the present-value formula for a stream of future cash flows.
49. Define risk premium.
50. In the loanable-funds model, what shifts the supply curve right?
51. How does a government budget deficit affect r and I in loanable-funds?

## Section G — International Trade (Ch. 18) — 4 questions

52. Define comparative advantage and contrast it with absolute advantage.
53. State the gains-from-trade rule for terms of trade.
54. Draw and label the welfare effects of a tariff (verbal description).
55. Distinguish tariff revenue from quota rent.

## Section H — International Finance (Ch. 19) — 5 questions

56. Define nominal exchange rate (state your convention).
57. If the dollar appreciates, what happens to US exports and imports?
58. State purchasing-power parity in one line.
59. Explain how a current-account deficit relates to a capital-account surplus.
60. If the US runs $S < I$, what does the open-economy identity imply about NX?
