🖨️ Print Packs

Take-anywhere PDF versions of every cram sheet & key reference

Master index of printable study packs across all three courses

Print these. Carry them. Annotate them by hand. Each pack below is laid out for a clean browser “Save as PDF” — nav and chrome are hidden, contrast is forced to black-on-white, and major sections start on fresh pages.

Tip

How to print: click any 🖨️ button to open the page, then Cmd-P (Mac) or Ctrl-P (Windows) → Save as PDF. The print stylesheet kicks in automatically.

Master print order

Read the printed packs in this sequence on the runup to your finals:

  1. Two weeks out — print all three consolidated print packs (links below). Annotate them as you study from the on-screen versions.
  2. One week out — print each course’s individual cram sheet / cheat sheet for spaced-repetition rehearsal.
  3. Exam morning — re-read only the cram sheet (IR 362 / ECON 205) or the Day-Of Playbook (ACCT 410x).
